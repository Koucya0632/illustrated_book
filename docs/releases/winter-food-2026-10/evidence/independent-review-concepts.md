# winter-2026-03 獨立概念審查

結論：8 詞選詞方案通過，僅限概念與去重；內容／圖片／音檔／發布程式尚未送審。

- `oden`：既有oden-warmer的英日釋義明確是分隔加熱設備；新詞為高湯煮成的食物，不同實體。圖片應以一碗具材與高湯表現成品，不畫保溫設備。 Link oden-warmer; ingredients may link the five reviewed food nouns.
- `steamed-meat-bun`：既有steamed-bun-warmer是透明蒸氣展示設備；hot-spring-bun是通常豆餡的伴手禮甜饅頭，均非鹹肉餡中華包。中華まん是更大的分類，不另新增其泛稱。 Typical pork may be noted without declaring all meat buns contain only pork; show pale pleated bun and visible cooked meat filling.
- `roasted-sweet-potato`：研究snapshot未出現同義詞。概念是烤熟甘藷而非生甘藷或普通馬鈴薯；官方零售商FAQ表明並非各地各店供應。 Show red-purple skin and broken golden cooked flesh; qualify availability.
- `daikon-radish`：研究snapshot沒有大根／daikon／radish詞條；可新增通用食材，不另為關東煮厚切蘿蔔立同義詞。 Generic vegetable definition and raw white root image; note/example may explain thick cooked oden rounds.
- `egg`：既有egg-carton是有凹槽保護雞蛋的包裝盒；API卵命中的furikake與mayonnaise只是含蛋的調味品提及，均非雞蛋本身。 Generic chicken egg definition and intact shell image. Oden use is usually a peeled boiled egg; do not define all eggs as boiled eggs.
- `chikuwa`：研究snapshot及API沒有同義詞。管狀魚漿食品可與一般魚漿製品區分；中心通孔是辨識特徵，不等於竹輪麩或香腸。 Show browned fish-paste tube with visible continuous hole, not solid sausage or wheat-gluten chikuwa-bu.
- `hanpen`：研究snapshot及API沒有同義詞。紀文說明以白身魚漿、卵白、山藥等含氣形成蓬鬆食感，與硬豆腐或切片蘿蔔不同。 Show soft white puffed square/triangle fish cake with fine porous texture; do not claim oden is its only use.
- `konjac`：研究snapshot及API沒有同義詞。日本こんにゃく協會將板蒟蒻與其他形態區分；本圖採灰黑細點板／三角塊是可辨的常見形式。 Definition covers konjac food, not raw plant, noodles only, or fruit dessert jelly; do not claim every konjac is grey or triangular.
